About LUMIX

Locally owned. Serious about the details.

LUMIX Outdoor Lighting is a North Jersey company based in Fair Lawn. We started it because a beautifully lit home shouldn't cost you a weekend on a ladder, a tangle of mismatched strands, or a January spent taking it all down.

Every project starts with your home's architecture. We plan where the light should fall, install it cleanly, keep it working through the season, and take it all down when the holidays are over.
